Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Controller

This app is operated privately by the owner of the TikTok account @diefussballchronik.

2. What data is processed

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives from TikTok an access token and basic

account information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the scope user.info.basic. The app uploads

the owner's own video files and captions via the scopes video.upload and video.publish.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens and account information are stored only locally on the owner's computer. They are not

sent to any third party and are not stored on any server.

4. Other users

The app has no other users and collects no data about other TikTok users or viewers.

5. Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app

permissions). Local tokens are then deleted.

6. Contact

Questions: via direct message to @diefussballchronik on TikTok.
